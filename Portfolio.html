About Me

Graphic Designer & Front-End Developer

Hi! I’m a college student who designs and builds for the web. I create clean visuals like logos, banners, and social media graphics in Illustrator, Figma, and Canva, then turn designs into responsive websites with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP.
